Assignment & Presentation Notes

Prompt: Describe real-world applications that require queues as a data structure, explain why queues are necessary, and choose one application to develop.

1. Real-World Applications

  • Printer Spooling: Queuing documents to print in order.
  • Call Centers: Holding incoming customer calls (FIFO).
  • Pharmacy Dispensing: Patients waiting in line for prescriptions.

2. Why Queues Are Necessary

  • Fairness: Strict First-In, First-Out (FIFO) chronological service.
  • Buffering: Absorbing workload spikes when demand exceeds speed.
  • Decoupling: Separating task submission from execution.

3. Developed Application

  • Pharmacy Queue & Sales System: Enqueues patients, dequeues them sequentially, records sales, and prints receipts with signatures.
